[Exact contact reference의 CRT 배치 문장 교체]

CRT ground center is A+9/8 f; its projected base is P(A+9/8 f,18),
with an 18px screen height and monitor top 36. Keyboard hand contacts
remain at H=P(A+3/8 f,18), including the frozen paired source pixels.
This changes the CRT center from A+3/4 f; it does not change chair A,
pelvis C, hand H, desk height 18, cushion height 12, chair back top 21,
32x64 character cells, seatAnchor (16,44), or the <=1px contact tolerance.
Reproject all four orientations from the same physical model.

Relative to the previous CRT position, apply ground-grid deltas:
desk SE (-3/8,0), SW (0,-3/8), NE (0,+3/8), NW (+3/8,0).
Their projected pixel deltas are (-12,-6), (+12,-6), (-12,+6), (+12,+6).
Update monitor/screen ordinary depthAnchor values by those grid deltas;
retain their physical ground sorting and existing isoZOrder values.
Rebuild previously hidden desktop pixels, CRT base/housing, screen/LED
holes and masks, all desk states, empty references and affected shadows.
Do not ship the design-review PNG translation as finished furniture.

When adopting this change in the exact contact fixture as well, synchronize
tools/iso_check.py reference generation (both .75 coefficients in the CRT
project call become 1.125) and regenerate assets/art/iso/ref/iso-contact-v1.png.
Those are future integration changes; this review modifies neither file.

[Naming and manifest conventions의 seat ordering 뒤 추가]

For NE/NW desks with SE/SW seated workers, split the keyboard-bearing
seat strip into horizontal desktop/keyboard and physical fascia/trim.
Assign the horizontal seat surface to rear isoZOrder -20 at the attached
worker's seat-ground depth; retain fascia at 10. Preserve source anchors,
frozen C/H contacts, ordinary per-edge depthAnchor values and exact empty
reconstruction through disjoint physical masks. The existing SE/SW desk
surface split for NW/NE seated workers remains in force.

Keep CRT housing/base in the ordinary physical furniture queue. Do not
raise the entire actor, or redraw hands/forearms over the CRT to force
visibility. If actor parts are later introduced, publish their physical
depth rules and transition behavior before relying on them.

[A3 / C2 접점 시각 검수 문구 추가]

For every pilot, facing and all four seated-typing-front phases, publish
visible/total counts for the documented contact-skin ROI and forearm/sleeve
ROI using the actual current furniture parts and queue. Define ROIs before
observing furniture overlap and preserve denominators across proposals.
Contact texels alone do not establish readable hands or a readable keyboard.
Review at native 1x and exact nearest-neighbor 3x: sleeves/forearms should
lead continuously toward the keyboard or into a physically valid occluder.

A static design-review proxy for the relocated CRT exposed 100% of the
contact-skin ROI and 86-96% of the forearm/sleeve ROI for SE/SW workers.
These figures are candidate effects, not final raster or runtime acceptance.
After physical furniture reconstruction, remeasure all four facings, retain
the existing >=85% NW/NE and >=90% SE/SW head gates, and review empty/state
reconstruction, props, walkers, sit/stand transitions, WPF depth and picking.
The hero is a composition reference and does not supply a numerical hand gate.

[CRT 배치를 유지하는 대안 — 위 9/8 변경을 채택하지 않을 때만]

With the retained CRT center A+3/4 f, SE/SW contact skin may be fully
occluded by the CRT housing in a correct 2:1 projection. Report that 0%
visibility explicitly. Do not describe it as successful visible-hand
typing, or as hands necessarily lying below the desktop. Judge typing
readability from continuous visible sleeves/forearms entering the occluder.
The actual hero shows light wrist/palm-like masses near a keyboard, without
an unambiguous pair of articulated hands; it cannot justify a 100% hand gate.

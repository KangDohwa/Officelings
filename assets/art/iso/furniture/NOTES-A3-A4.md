# FURNITURE-1 — A3 + A4 handoff

Made six families, each with SE/SW/NE/NW native variants: warm wood desk + cream CRT, teal chair, red three-cushion sofa, blue woven rug, wood TV cabinet with play icon, and side table. A3 is front profile only. Desk default aliases off; working has four distinct 3fps screen frames; screensaver is static. Rear screens expose only a status LED. Chair has exactly two disjoint rear/front clips per facing. Desk and sofa use ground-cell/edge strips, with occupied seat groups. Five families have separate contact/cast shadow sheets; rug is nonblocking floor furniture. Sofa nap approaches/contact offsets are explicit and rotated.

Source manifests are in workstations/ and lounge/. `furniture-part-convention.md` publishes anchors, dimensions, part roles, links and depth keys. Root reviews: `furniture-review.png` (all24 views, exact2×), `furniture-fixture-room.png` (S0 furniture subset, exact2×, three placeholder seated rigs), `furniture-contact-review.png` (four exact S0 contacts,4×), and `furniture-overlap-review.png` (near/far/end walkers and occupied sofas,2×). Empty reconstruction PNGs and screen masks are beside their manifests. No flat source art, pack, shared code, docs or tests were changed. No commit.

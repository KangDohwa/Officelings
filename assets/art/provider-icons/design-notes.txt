Original provider icon design notes

Codex: a teal terminal and >_ prompt communicate code and command-line work. The silhouette is a wide rounded rectangle, with no interlocking knot or floral loops.
Claude: an amber speech bubble with a small ink dot and a diagonal pen stroke communicates conversation and writing. Its left tail is not radial or asterisk-shaped; the yellow-amber fill is paired with blue-green ink, not an orange-on-tan brand treatment.
Antigravity: a violet balloon, loose tether and quiet ground line communicate lift. Its tall rounded silhouette is neither a four-point sparkle nor a gradient star. All shading uses discrete flat pixel colors.
Other agent: a slate robot head with ears and an antenna is a friendly generic fallback.
Supervisor: a small gold crown marks responsibility. Sub-agent: a navy lanyard with a paper ID marks a supporting agent. Both transparent badges share the provider canvas and can be overlaid at (0,0).

Every 10/12/16/24/32px image has its own authored pixel map. Only @2x/@3x exports repeat pixels. Small versions simplify detail intentionally.
Limits: the pen is only a two-pixel ink gesture at 10px; the balloon tether and ground line are one pixel wide. Badges partly cover the upper/lower right of icons. Prefer unbadged 10px or 12px icons for the cleanest provider silhouette. Color-vision matrices are approximate visual checks, not accessibility certification.
No official artwork or source images were used. These generic object metaphors avoid the forbidden logo motifs. Runtime integration is outside this asset-only task.
